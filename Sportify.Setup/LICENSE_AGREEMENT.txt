ABOUT SPORTIFY

Sportify is an open source project of the
"Digital Tools and Methods - Group of Sports and Gardens":
computational and facade design students of TH OWL (Technische Hochschule
Ostwestfalen-Lippe), School of Architecture, developed in the context of the
MID project. The students come from architecture and engineering backgrounds.
The main partner of the project is GOLDBECK.

The Revit add-in and the web app that comes with it were developed with the main
assistance of Claude (Anthropic), and led by the computational design team:
Nada, Moamen, Sukriti and Ali.

SOURCE CODE

  Web app                https://github.com/nfe15rajab-bot/sportfify_goldbeck

WHAT YOU MAY DO

Sportify is open source and is released for educational purposes. You may
install and use it, read it, copy it and change it, for study, teaching and
research, as long as you keep this notice and the names above.
